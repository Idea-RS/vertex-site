# Part icons — provenance

The eight part figures in the industries section (`src/components/drawing/PartIcons.tsx`) are not invented icons. Each is an orthographic figure traced from one sheet of a United States patent granted between 1925 and 1947, redrawn in the site's linework at 240 × 160: vx-100 outlines, vx-400 centrelines, witness lines and hidden detail, 45° hatching where the source figure is a section. The tracing keeps the source figure's view, proportions and features; reference numerals, leaders and captions are dropped, and small features below the icon's resolution (thread crests, screw slots, radii under 2 px) are simplified.

Every source was downloaded as the official PDF from the USPTO (`https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/<number>`), page 1 rasterised, the figure cropped and enlarged 2× for tracing. Candidates were found by web search restricted to patents.google.com, then checked against the PDF before use.

## What changed from rev 4

Rev 4's eight figures were drawn from memory of what such parts look like. Two of them (the gusseted bracket and the stub axle) had no source at all and the rest were loose. Rev 5 replaced all eight. Where the earlier name did not match a real figure, the name changed: "Gusseted bracket" became a welded seat bracket, "Volute casing" a pump casing rear view, "Stator lamination" a laminated core, "Lug fitting" a tube-end lug fitting. No segment was left with an invented figure.
