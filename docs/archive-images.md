# Archive thumbnails — provenance

The Find scene's receding field of sheets is built from `public/archive/*.webp`. The folder is the only integration point: any images dropped in become the archive.

Every file is sheet 1 of the drawings of a United States patent granted between 1916 and 1960, downloaded as the official PDF from the USPTO (`https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/<number>`), page 1 rasterised, trimmed of scanner edge bars and page margins, converted to grey, softened by 0.4 px, tinted to a warm paper white and resized to 480 px wide (WebP q78). Nothing was cleaned or re-traced. Candidates were found by searching Google Patents for bearing housings, pump casings, valve bodies, gear cases and pipe couplings with US priority dates 1900–1960.
